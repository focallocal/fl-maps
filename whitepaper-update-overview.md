# Whitepaper Update & Evaluation Overview

This document will serve as a working overview of the changes and considerations for updating the whitepaper. It will be used to evaluate the existing whitepaper and guide the update process.

---

## Overview of Planned Changes

### Last Resort Rip-Cord

Rewarding action to create a kinder world with a crypto token is largely uncharted territory and challenges for the community and core team to overcome are guaranteed.

It is possible that we will be unable to prevent AI and scammers from gaming the system, or that the community will be focused mainly on greed and form into factionality, rewarding each other only.

There is a non-zero chance that the tokenisation will have a detrimental impact on the original mission to create a world with a little more kindness and well-being in it.

If it's clear that has occurred and our best efforts to solve it have failed, we reserve the right to, as a last resort, detach the crypto-currency from the PHM platform (and any of the other project's platforms), removing the crypto-incentive entirely from them and leaving the platform(s) for their kindness-based original mission only, and the tokens to become their own separate entity.

The token-reward mechanism would obviously change in this scenario, and likely cease to exist. Or the community could vote on, and build, a new project to reward kindness online away from our platforms.

##Community Tokens
###Token allocation

The platform makes a fixed monthly allocation of community tokens available to eligible active members. Eligibility is based on general participation and time active within the community and does not depend on the completion or performance of any specific task, service, or contribution.

Eligible members may claim a monthly token allowance corresponding to their trust-level band. Trust levels are determined by objective criteria such as account age and general activity thresholds and do not represent an evaluation of merit, quality, or value of any individual action.
Token use and transfers

Once claimed, tokens may be voluntarily transferred by members to other members as expressions of appreciation or support. The platform does not assign, recommend, direct, or guarantee token transfers for any activity or behavior.

The platform does not measure, rank, or reward contributions using tokens and does not promise or imply that any action will result in receiving tokens.
No compensation or remuneration

Community tokens do not constitute payment, compensation, income, consideration, or remuneration for services or activities of any kind, including but not limited to hosting, accommodation, hospitality, moderation, or participation in community activities.

The platform does not provide rewards or incentives for specific user actions.
